[The Encanto Neighborhood Field Experiments Dataset: Land–Atmosphere Interactions in Urban Phoenix](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-026-08464-w) is now published in Scientific Data! This collaboration brings together observations of Phoenix's urban microclimate and methods for reconstructing temperature fields.
