Three new preprints on the arXiv! [Wiener-space cubature for nonlinear Gaussian filtering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04041) uses tools from rough path theory to derive new, efficient filtering algorithms (led by my amazing intern Luke Snow); [simulation-free learning of population dynamics with Wasserstein Lagrangian residuals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03679) extends our stitching approach to general Lagrangian actions in population dynamics; and [Slaying the Hydra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04017) uses tools from actual causality to learn about interactions in large language models.
