I'm a postdoctoral research scientist at [Basis](https://basis.ai), a non-profit research institute, and a Research Affiliate at LIDS, MIT, working with [Matt Levine](https://mattlevine.netlify.app/) (Basis) and [Youssef Marzouk](https://aeroastro.mit.edu/people/youssef-m-marzouk/) (MIT). I previously completed by PhD at [Stony Brook University](https://www.stonybrook.edu/) working with [Petar Djurić](https://sites.google.com/stonybrook.edu/petardjuric/), where my dissertation focused on online and sequential Bayesian learning, with applications in ensembles and experimental design. I'm largely interested in Bayesian machine learning and statistics, causal inference, and dynamical systems, and especially like the many intersections of these topics. These days, I'm mostly thinking about dynamical systems, including post-Bayesian inference, Bayesian workflow, and new computational methods.

During Summer 2025, I was a research intern [Basis](https://basis.ai), where I worked on problems in applied Bayesian ML and causality. During my PhD, I was a member of the [Southwest Integrated Field Laboratory](https://sw-ifl.asu.edu/people), where I worked with [Katia Lamer](https://katialamer.weebly.com/) in applying ML techniques to applied experimental design problems. You can find more about my research interests in publications on my [research page](research). Prior to graduate school, I also completed my undergrad at Stony Brook in math and statistics.

I'm active in developing and contributing to open-source software. With Matt Levine, I created [dynestyx](https://github.com/dynestyx), a probabilistic programming language built on NumPyro for dynamical systems inference. I am a maintainer of [cd_dynamax](https://github.com/hd-UQ/cd_dynamax), a library for continuous-discrete filtering and smoothing, and a contributor to [cuthbert](https://github.com/state-space-models/cuthbert), a generic library for state-space inference.
